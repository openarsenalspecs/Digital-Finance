# PublicLaunch
**Quantifying Market Behavior.**
- HTML Mirror:  [https://roxanneardary.com/publiclaunch-specification/](https://roxanneardary.com/publiclaunch-specification/)  

---

PublicLaunch is an open-source AI-powered market intelligence platform designed to quantify market behavior through historical analysis, hype detection, manipulation-risk assessment, IPO research, cryptocurrency launch monitoring, and probabilistic outcome modeling.

The platform helps researchers, investors, journalists, analysts, educators, and regulators identify unusual market activity, compare current events to historical patterns, and evaluate market behavior using transparent data-driven methodologies. PublicLaunch focuses on research, analytics, and market transparency rather than investment recommendations or trading signals.

## Objectives

- Quantify market behavior using historical data.
- Detect potential pump-and-dump activity.
- Analyze IPOs and public market launches.
- Monitor cryptocurrency launches and ecosystem risks.
- Measure social hype and sentiment trends.
- Generate probabilistic outcome models.
- Improve market transparency through open-source research.
- Provide explainable AI-driven market intelligence.
