# Nexa: Continuity Engine

**Nexa: Continuity Engine** is a generational wealth operating system designed to model, simulate, and govern multi-generational financial structures through programmable trust logic, lifecycle-aware rules, and multi-trust network architecture.

It is not a financial advisor or custodial service. It is a deterministic infrastructure layer for structuring, simulating, and governing long-term wealth systems.

---

## Core Concept

Nexa transforms traditional estate and trust structures into a programmable system that can:

- Define how wealth is governed
- Simulate how it evolves over time
- Control how it is distributed across generations
- Model real-world life events and economic conditions
- Coordinate multiple trusts as a unified system

---

## Full Feature List

### 1. Trust Rule Engine
A programmable rule system that governs all trust behavior.

- HEMS rule modeling (Health, Education, Maintenance, Support)
- Age-based distribution logic
- Trustee discretionary controls
- Conditional eligibility rules
- Asset-specific distribution constraints
- Approval workflows and multi-signature logic

---

### 2. Time Engine (Generational Simulation Layer)
A predictive financial simulation system for long-term planning.

- Multi-decade wealth projection (10–100+ years)
- Inflation and macroeconomic scenario modeling
- Asset growth simulation (stocks, real estate, crypto)
- Bitcoin volatility and compounding models
- Inheritance cycle simulation across generations
- Tax exposure forecasting (estate, gift, GST)
- Scenario comparison engine (best case / baseline / stress test)

---

### 3. Multi-Trust Network Layer
A system for managing interconnected trust ecosystems.

- Hierarchical dynasty trust structures
- Sub-trust specialization (education, crypto, real estate, etc.)
- Cross-trust dependency mapping
- Shared beneficiary modeling across trusts
- Isolation of risk between asset pools
- Trust-to-trust governance relationships

---

### 4. Life Event System
A dynamic system that adapts trust behavior to real-world events.

- Birth and generational entry events
- Education completion triggers
- Marriage and divorce conditions
- Business creation and entrepreneurship triggers
- Disability, emergency, and hardship conditions
- Death and inheritance transition events
- Event-based rule activation system

---

### 5. Identity Permissions Layer
A secure role-based access control system.

- Beneficiary role (view + request access)
- Trustee role (approve + execute distributions)
- Co-trustee governance structures
- Auditor read-only compliance access
- Advisor limited-visibility roles
- Permission-based rule modification controls
- Multi-signature approval requirements

---

### 6. Asset Layer (Traditional + Digital)
Unified asset tracking and governance system.

- Fiat cash pools
- Investment portfolios
- Real estate holdings
- LLC ownership structures
- Bitcoin and cryptocurrency assets
- Custodian-linked or multi-sig crypto tracking
- Asset-level rule binding

---

### 7. Tax & Exemption Tracking Engine
A financial compliance and modeling system.

- Lifetime gift exemption tracking
- Estate tax exposure modeling
- Generation-skipping transfer (GST) tracking
- Asset basis tracking (including crypto)
- Gift reporting simulation (Form 709 support logic)
- Jurisdiction-aware tax modeling

---

### 8. Governance & Policy Layer
Defines how the system itself is controlled.

- Family council voting logic
- Trustee replacement rules
- Amendment procedures for trust rules
- Conflict resolution structures
- Policy versioning and historical state tracking

---

### 9. Investment Policy Engine
Controls how assets can be managed and invested.

- Risk profile configuration (conservative, moderate, aggressive)
- Asset allocation constraints
- Crypto exposure limits
- Liquidity requirements
- Rebalancing rules
- Investment restriction policies

---

### 10. Audit & Compliance Layer
A transparent and verifiable system log.

- Immutable event logging design
- Hash-chained audit trails
- Trustee action history
- Distribution verification logs
- Compliance reporting outputs
- Full system traceability

---

## Design Principles

Nexa is built on three core principles:

- **Continuity over transactions**
- **Structure over discretion**
- **Simulation over assumption**

---

## System Behavior Model

Nexa operates through four core processes:

1. **Define** – establish trust structure and rules  
2. **Simulate** – project outcomes across time  
3. **Execute** – apply rule-based distributions  
4. **Audit** – record and verify all actions  

---

## Security Model

- No autonomous fund custody by default  
- Trustee-authorized execution required  
- Role-based permission enforcement  
- Immutable audit logging  
- Deterministic rule evaluation  

---
