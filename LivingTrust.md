# LivingTrust Standard

## A Foundation for Every Generation

LivingTrust Standard is an AGPL-3.0+ open source specification for a modular living trust architecture designed around financial stability, capital preservation, predictable income, individualized cost-of-living support, deterministic beneficiary succession, and generational continuity.

The specification establishes a common architecture that can be independently implemented for an individual's living trust and subsequently reused as a standardized template for independently established trusts for family members. Each trust remains legally and financially independent while following the same core specification.

LivingTrust Standard is an architectural specification and does not itself create a legally enforceable trust. Actual trust instruments, trustee appointments, beneficiary provisions, transfers, tax treatment, and administration must comply with applicable law and be established through appropriate legal and financial professionals.

## Design Principles

- Independent trust implementation
- Modular architecture
- Stable and predictable income
- Capital preservation
- No speculative investment strategy
- No unnecessary financial risk
- Sustainable monthly distributions
- Deterministic beneficiary selection
- Equal beneficiary allocation where specified
- Annual residency determination
- State-based cost-of-living calculations
- AI-assisted research and verification
- Human and legally authorized trust administration
- IRS gift-giving compliance
- Transparent calculations and records
- Generational continuity
- Reusable trust architecture
- Open source implementation under AGPL-3.0+

### Minor and Legally Incapable Beneficiary Module

The Minor and Legally Incapable Beneficiary Module governs payment administration when the qualified beneficiary cannot directly receive trust payments.

Features include:

- Direct payment to legally capable adult beneficiaries
- Guardian-managed payments for beneficiaries under 18
- Guardian-managed payments for legally incapable beneficiaries
- Legal guardian verification
- Legal representative verification
- Beneficiary ownership preservation
- Joint custody payment administration
- Mother-designated payment recipient when parents have joint custody, subject to applicable law and court orders

A guardian or authorized representative receives payments for administration on behalf of the beneficiary and does not become the underlying beneficiary solely by receiving the payment.

### New Generation Access Module

The New Generation Access Module provides a fallback beneficiary mechanism when no qualifying family beneficiaries exist.

Features include:

- Fallback beneficiary selection
- New Year Beneficiary Window
- January 1 commencement
- Twelve-month identification period
- Chronological birth ranking
- Official birth record verification
- Household income verification
- Government-declared poverty-level eligibility
- Equal beneficiary allocation
- Deterministic selection
- Closed annual beneficiary determination
- No retroactive beneficiary additions
- Distribution after completion of the determination period

The New Year Beneficiary Window begins at exactly 12:00:00 AM on January 1 of each calendar year and remains open for one year for the identification and verification of qualifying children.

No funds are released during the determination period.

Eligible births are ranked chronologically according to authoritative birth records. Once the determination period closes, the qualifying beneficiary list becomes final for that annual cycle.

Birth records or qualifying beneficiaries not discovered and verified within the one-year determination period cannot be retroactively added to that completed annual beneficiary class.

Every qualifying child receives an equal trust allocation from the designated beneficiary funds until those funds are depleted, subject to the applicable trust rules and law.

### Annual Residency Module

The Annual Residency Module determines the state used for each beneficiary's annual cost-of-living calculation.

Features include:

- Mandatory annual residency declaration
- State of residence declaration before January 1
- Annual residency record
- State-based cost-of-living assignment
- Fixed annual residency classification
- Residency verification
- Annual recalculation eligibility

Each beneficiary must declare their primary state of residence for the upcoming calendar year before January 1.

The declared state governs the beneficiary's cost-of-living calculation for that calendar year.

### Cost of Living Module

The Cost of Living Module establishes a standardized state-level calculation for monthly beneficiary support.

Features include:

- State-level averages
- Two-bedroom apartment rental rate
- Residential utility costs
- Internet service included within utilities
- Electricity
- Water and sewer
- Natural gas or other household energy
- Trash service
- Automobile payment
- Automobile insurance
- Food
- Clothing
- Gasoline and transportation fuel
- Monthly cost-of-living baseline
- Annual cost-of-living dataset
- State-specific distribution calculations
- Dataset versioning

The baseline calculation uses:

Two-bedroom apartment rent + utilities including internet + automobile payment + automobile insurance + food + clothing + gasoline and transportation fuel = monthly cost-of-living baseline.

The specification uses state averages rather than requiring reimbursement of individual beneficiary expenses.

## Deterministic Administration

The specification favors predetermined rules over discretionary decision-making.

The following should be deterministic wherever legally and technically practical:

- Beneficiary succession
- Beneficiary ranking
- Equal allocation
- Annual residency classification
- Cost-of-living calculations
- Distribution calculations
- Annual beneficiary determination
- New Year Beneficiary Window
- Gift compliance calculations
- Dataset version selection

AI systems may research, verify, calculate, and report, but may not rewrite the governing rules of a trust.

## Generational Continuity

LivingTrust Standard is designed to provide a repeatable architecture that can continue across generations.

The specification supports:

- Individual financial stability
- Independent family trust creation
- Consistent trust architecture
- Beneficiary succession
- Generational beneficiary transitions
- Equal beneficiary treatment
- Cost-of-living-adjusted support
- Long-term capital preservation
- Sustainable distributions
- New-generation fallback beneficiaries

Every newly established trust follows the same core guidelines, creating a consistent framework while preserving the legal and financial independence of each trust.
