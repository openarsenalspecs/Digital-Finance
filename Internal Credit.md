# Internal Credit

**Your Financial Co-Pilot For Self-Borrowing.**

Internal Credit is an AI-powered, modular financial platform that enables account holders to borrow against their own savings while continuing to earn returns on their deposited capital. The platform combines self-collateralized lending, intelligent financial guidance, wealth preservation tools, and modern banking infrastructure into a single extensible system.

The core concept allows users to borrow up to 70% of their account value while keeping their savings invested and growing. Loan interest is credited back to the account owner, creating a financial model focused on preserving and growing wealth rather than extracting it.

## Core Principles

- Borrow against your own capital.
- Maintain growth on deposited funds.
- Maximum borrowing limit of 70% of account value.
- Interest paid on loans is credited back to the account owner.
- AI-powered financial guidance and planning.
- Modular architecture for banks, credit unions, cooperatives, and fintech platforms.
- Wealth preservation and long-term financial sustainability.
