## Purpose

CryptoCradle is an open-source, global startup accelerator and funding infrastructure designed to provide an alternative to centralized and profit-focused startup accelerator models.

The platform is designed around community governance, transparent financial infrastructure, privacy, security, global accessibility, fractional investment, founder support, and open-source development.

CryptoCradle shall provide infrastructure that enables founders to develop companies, raise capital, connect with investors and mentors, manage milestones, and participate in a transparent funding ecosystem.

The platform shall be modular, extensible, vendor-neutral, self-hostable, and capable of supporting multiple deployment models without requiring dependence on a single financial institution, blockchain, identity provider, cloud provider, AI provider, or payment processor.

## Financial Safety

CryptoCradle shall distinguish between software functionality and regulated financial activity.

The platform shall provide configurable compliance controls for securities, payments, cryptocurrency, investment activity, KYC, AML, taxation, and cross-border transactions.

No feature shall assume that an investment, security, token, transfer, fundraising campaign, or financial product is legally available in every jurisdiction.

Human review shall remain available for high-risk financial, compliance, security, and governance decisions.
