## 1️⃣ Project Overview

CryptoCradle is an **open-source, global startup accelerator** designed to democratize access to early-stage funding and foster a secure, AI-assisted, fractional-investment ecosystem.  

**Key Goals:**  
- Enable **fractional micro-investments** in startups worldwide.  
- Ensure **end-to-end encryption, KYC compliance, and blockchain-based equity tracking**.  
- Provide **AI tools** for founder evaluation, investor insights, and mentorship matching.  
- Foster a **community-driven ecosystem** with DAO governance, gamification, and collaboration tools.  

For detailed compliance, security, and attribution, see [notice.md](./notice.md).
