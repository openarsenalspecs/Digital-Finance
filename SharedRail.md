# SharedRail

**Global by Default.**

SharedRail is an open source, federated payment network that enables verified individuals and organizations to send and receive money using a verified email address. Designed as open financial infrastructure rather than a proprietary payment platform, SharedRail supports instant, encrypted, multi-currency payments with mandatory identity verification, two-factor authentication, and AI-assisted human intent verification.

Licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)**, SharedRail is built as a modular platform that anyone can audit, extend, self-host, and improve while ensuring that network-deployed improvements remain open to the community.

## Objectives

- Build an open financial infrastructure for the world.
- Make global payments as simple as sending an email.
- Eliminate proprietary platform lock-in.
- Protect users through encryption and AI-assisted scam prevention.
- Support instant cross-border settlement.
- Enable modular deployment for institutions of any size.
- Provide transparent, auditable, and secure payment infrastructure.
