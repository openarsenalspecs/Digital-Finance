# Wealth Continuum

**Following the Stewards of Capital.**

Wealth Continuum is an AGPL-3.0+ licensed, AI-powered research platform designed to identify, analyze, and monitor private banks, credit unions, mutual institutions, cooperatives, employee-owned businesses, family-owned businesses, and other privately held organizations that demonstrate transparency, financial strength, responsible governance, and long-term wealth preservation characteristics.

Unlike traditional investment research platforms that primarily focus on public markets and short-term performance, Wealth Continuum evaluates institutions through the lens of stewardship, ownership quality, transparency, institutional resilience, and generational wealth continuity.

## Mission

Wealth Continuum helps individuals, families, researchers, and communities discover organizations that demonstrate responsible stewardship of capital and the ability to preserve wealth across generations.

The platform combines financial analysis, document intelligence, ownership evaluation, governance review, AI-powered risk detection, and historical research to provide a comprehensive picture of institutional quality.
